UX Newsletter Issue 2: Documentation AI, scheduling intelligence, and intake workflow improvements.
UX Newsletter | Issue 2 June 30, 2026
Needle-free, simpler experiences built for real-world care
Narrative documentation, now AI-assisted
The team introduced AI-generated visit summaries that support clinicians with faster, more structured documentation while preserving clinical review and control.
This flow includes draft generation states, reviewable narratives, and completion checkpoints to keep quality and compliance at the center.
Visit summary workflow evolution
From blank narrative entry to AI-generated draft and signature-ready completion, the new flow keeps required sections visible and reduces documentation burden.
 
 
 
Clinical confidence through guided alerts and structured inputs
UX patterns now emphasize clear intervention prompts, scoring transparency, and structured decision support across assessment workflows.
 
 
 
Scheduling intelligence and team coordination updates
Care coordination experiences continue to improve with clearer map-based assignment, shift routing, and workload visibility patterns.
 
 
 
Intake and billing workflow simplification
Improved filter patterns, customizable views, and billing steps make referral and intake operations easier to manage at scale.
 
 
 
Meet the people behind the work
Cross-functional collaboration across UX, product, and engineering continues to accelerate delivery quality in every release.
